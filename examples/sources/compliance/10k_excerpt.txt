PART I

ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Northwind Logistics Corporation ("Northwind," "the Company," "we," "us," or "our") is a technology-enabled
third-party logistics provider that offers freight brokerage, dedicated contract carriage, and managed
transportation services to customers across the United States and Canada. We were founded in 2012 and became
a public company in 2021. Our principal services include (i) truckload and less-than-truckload freight
brokerage, (ii) dedicated fleet operations under long-term transportation agreements, (iii) intermodal
brokerage connecting rail and truck networks, and (iv) supply chain analytics and visibility software offered
on a software-as-a-service basis.

We generate revenue primarily from customer contracts that are secured through our direct sales force and a
network of independent agent representatives. No single customer accounted for more than 8% of our revenue in
fiscal 2024. As of December 31, 2024, we employed 1,248 people, of whom 612 were professional staff and 636
were drivers and operational personnel.

Our industry is highly competitive. We compete with national asset-based carriers, large freight brokerages,
and digital freight marketplaces. Key competitive factors include price, network density, carrier reliability,
technology, and service quality. We believe our differentiated analytics platform, which we market under the
trade name "HarborIQ," provides us with a meaningful advantage in carrier selection and lane profitability.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

Investing in our common stock involves a high degree of risk. In addition to the other information contained
in this Annual Report on Form 10-K, you should carefully consider the risks described below.

Risks Related to Our Business and Industry

Our operating results depend on the volume of freight we move and the rates we are able to charge, both of
which are subject to significant cyclical volatility. Downturns in general economic conditions, including
recessions, inflationary pressure, or disruptions to supply chains, have in the past materially reduced the
volume of freight available and compressed our margins. A sustained decline in macroeconomic conditions would
have a material adverse effect on our revenue, profitability, and cash flows.

We depend on third-party carriers to move freight, and any disruption in the capacity available in our
network, including reductions in the number of owner-operators, could impair our ability to serve customers.
If we are unable to secure sufficient carrier capacity at economical rates, our revenue and customer retention
may decline.

Litigation, regulatory enforcement, and evolving labor and employment laws may result in increased costs and
liabilities. Claims that we are a joint employer of our independent contractor carriers, or challenges to the
classification of independent contractors under state law, could expose us to material financial and
reputational harm.

Risks Related to Technology and Data

Our HarborIQ platform relies on the collection and processing of large volumes of data. A cybersecurity
incident, including a breach of the data of our customers or carriers, could disrupt our operations, result in
the loss or compromise of confidential information, and subject us to significant liability and regulatory
penalties. We maintain insurance coverage, but our coverage may be insufficient for the full scope of a
sophisticated attack.

Risks Related to Our Common Stock

Our stock price has been and may continue to be volatile. The market price of our common stock could fluctuate
significantly in response to quarterly variations in our operating results, changes in financial estimates by
securities analysts, general market conditions, and other factors beyond our control.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS

Overview

Fiscal 2024 was a year of disciplined execution for Northwind. We delivered $412.3 million in revenue, an
increase of 6.1% compared with $388.6 million in fiscal 2023, driven primarily by growth in dedicated contract
carriage and intermodal volume. Gross margin improved 90 basis points to 17.4%, reflecting better carrier
procurement and tighter lane management enabled by HarborIQ.

Results of Operations

Revenue. Revenue increased by $23.7 million, or 6.1%, to $412.3 million in fiscal 2024. Freight brokerage
revenue decreased 1.2% to $241.8 million on lower spot-market rates, while dedicated contract carriage revenue
increased 18.3% to $124.5 million due to the addition of 14 new dedicated fleet engagements. Intermodal and
other revenue increased 9.4% to $46.0 million.

Cost of revenue and gross margin. Cost of revenue increased 4.7% to $340.6 million, driven by increased
fuel costs and insurance premiums. As a result, gross margin expanded from 16.5% to 17.4%.

Selling, general, and administrative expenses. SG&A expenses increased 8.2% to $52.1 million, primarily due
to investments in our sales organization and technology headcount.

Net income. Net income for fiscal 2024 was $18.9 million, compared with $16.2 million in fiscal 2023. Diluted
earnings per share was $0.71, compared with $0.62 in the prior year.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

As of December 31, 2024, we held $63.4 million in cash and cash equivalents and $22.0 million available under
our revolving credit facility. Cash flows from operating activities were $27.1 million during fiscal 2024,
compared with $24.4 million in fiscal 2023. We believe our existing cash, cash equivalents, and available
borrowings are sufficient to meet our anticipated operating and capital expenditure requirements for at least
the next twelve months. We have no material off-balance sheet arrangements.
