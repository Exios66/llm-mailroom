CONFIDENTIAL - ATTORNEY-CLIENT PRIVILEGED
DUE DILIGENCE REPORT

Prepared for: Acquisition Committee, HarborPoint Holdings, Inc.
Re: Proposed Acquisition of Meridian Fulfillment Services, LLC
Date: November 12, 2024

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

We have completed our due diligence review of Meridian Fulfillment Services, LLC ("Meridian" or the
"Target"), a privately held third-party fulfillment operator headquartered in Reno, Nevada, in connection
with the proposed acquisition of 100% of the membership interests of the Target. Based on our review, we
recommend that the Acquisition Committee proceed with the transaction subject to the conditions and
reservations identified in Section VII below.

I. BUSINESS REVIEW

Meridian operates three fulfillment facilities (Reno, NV; Sparks, NV; and Phoenix, AZ) totaling approximately
420,000 square feet, and services 34 active customer accounts across e-commerce, healthcare, and direct-to-
consumer brands. Revenue for fiscal 2023 was $68.2 million, with an adjusted EBITDA of $9.4 million. The
Target's top five customers represent 71% of revenue, a concentration we flag as a material consideration.

II. LEGAL REVIEW

Litigation. The Target is a party to one active commercial dispute (Meridian v. PeakCarrier, Inc., U.S.
District Court, D. Nevada, Case No. 3:24-cv-0041), arising from a 2022 carrier-services dispute. Management
estimates potential exposure of $1.2 million; outside counsel assesses the Target's likelihood of success at
approximately 60%. The acquisition agreement should address allocation of any resulting liability.

Compliance. The Target is current on all federal, state, and local filings and taxes. No material citations
were identified in the last two years. The Phoenix facility holds an active use permit; the Reno and Sparks
facilities are in compliance with county land-use regulations.

Contracts. We reviewed the Target's ten largest customer contracts. Seven contain change-of-control
provisions requiring counterparty consent to assignment, which will require notices under Section 4.1 of the
proposed Asset Purchase Agreement.

III. FINANCIAL REVIEW

Adjusted EBITDA of $9.4 million reflects add-backs of $1.8 million in one-time restructuring charges and
$0.6 million in owner-operator compensation. Net working capital at September 30, 2024 was $4.1 million.
Capital expenditure requirements of approximately $2.0 million are projected for fiscal 2025 to support a new
conveyor system at the Phoenix facility.

IV. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The Target holds one registered trademark (MERIDIAN FULFILLMENT), two pending trademark applications, and
proprietary warehouse-management software code. We recommend confirming that all IP is assigned to the Target
and included in the asset purchase.

V. ENVIRONMENTAL, HEALTH, AND SAFETY

Phase I environmental assessments for all three facilities are clean, with no recognized environmental
conditions. Workers' compensation experience modifier of 0.87 reflects a favorable safety record.

VI. EMPLOYMENT AND LABOR

The Target employs 412 W-2 employees and engages 38 independent contractor drivers. No union or collective
bargaining agreements exist. We note that the classification of the independent contractor drivers has not
been the subject of any audit or claim to date.

VII. KEY FINDINGS AND RISK FLAGS

1. Customer concentration: 71% of revenue from five customers (HIGH).
2. Change-of-control consent requirements in seven customer contracts (MEDIUM).
3. Active litigation exposure estimated at up to $1.2 million (MEDIUM).
4. Independent contractor classification risk for 38 drivers (MEDIUM).
5. No environmental or compliance violations identified (LOW).

VIII. RECOMMENDATION

Proceed with the acquisition, subject to: (a) a purchase price adjustment to reflect the litigation exposure,
(b) obtaining counterparty consents for change-of-control provisions prior to closing, and (c) inclusion of
representations and warranties addressing contractor classification.
