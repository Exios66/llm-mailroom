# Correspondence — Extraction Skill

Extract: `sender`, `recipient`, `additional_recipients`, `communication_type`, `communication_date`, `key_points`, `demand_amount`, `action_items`, `urgency`, `referenced_communications`.

- A demand letter about a contract is correspondence, not a contract.
- `communication_date` is the date sent, not a referenced deadline.
- `demand_amount` of 0 is a stated amount.
- Neutral tone defaults to urgency `routine`, not null.
- Do not invent recipients from letterhead without an addressee line.
