UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS
OF
HARBORPOINT HOLDINGS, INC.

Pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, the undersigned, being all of the members
of the Board of Directors of HarborPoint Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation, hereby adopt the following
resolutions without a meeting, effective as of the date set forth below:

RESOLVED, that the Board of Directors hereby approves the entry into a Master Services Agreement with
Northwind Logistics Corporation, substantially in the form reviewed by the Board, and authorizes the Chief
Executive Officer to execute and deliver such agreement on behalf of the corporation;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Board of Directors hereby authorizes and directs the Chief Executive Officer to
take all such actions and execute all such documents as may be necessary or appropriate to carry out the
foregoing resolutions, including the incurrence of up to $1,500,000 in capital expenditures in connection
therewith;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Board of Directors hereby establishes the Audit Committee, consisting of directors
Amelia Rodriguez (Chair), Thomas Okafor, and Priya Nair, effective immediately;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Board of Directors hereby approves the grant of 50,000 restricted stock units to
the Chief Executive Officer, subject to the terms of the corporation's 2023 Equity Incentive Plan and the
execution of a restricted stock unit award agreement.

This written consent may be executed in one or more counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original
and all of which together shall constitute one instrument.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the undersigned have executed this Written Consent as of the 10th day of January, 2025.

/s/ Amelia Rodriguez
Amelia Rodriguez, Director

/s/ Thomas Okafor
Thomas Okafor, Director

/s/ Priya Nair
