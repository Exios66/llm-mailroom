MEMORANDUM

TO: Amelia Rodriguez, General Counsel
FROM: Dana Alvarez, Senior Associate
DATE: February 3, 2025
RE: Analysis of Remedies Under the Master Services Agreement with Northwind Logistics

Amelia:

You asked me to assess the remedies available to HarborPoint should Northwind fail to cure the service
deficiencies identified in our December 19, 2024 letter.

Background. Under the Master Services Agreement dated June 1, 2024 (the "Agreement"), Northwind provides
dedicated fleet and brokerage services to HarborPoint. Since October, HarborPoint has experienced on-time
performance of approximately 82%, below the 95% service-level commitment in Section 4.2 of the Agreement.
HarborPoint delivered formal notice of noncompliance on December 19, 2024.

Analysis. Section 9.4 of the Agreement establishes a ten-business-day cure period following notice of a
material breach. Northwind's cure window closes on February 6, 2025. If the cure is not effected, HarborPoint
may terminate the Agreement under Section 9.5 and seek damages under Section 9.8, which includes a
liquidated-damages provision of $2,500 per day of unmet service level, capped at $150,000.

Recommendation. I recommend that HarborPoint allow the cure period to expire without further forbearance,
then deliver a termination notice under Section 9.5 and simultaneously initiate the dispute-resolution
process under Article 11 (which provides for an initial mediation before arbitration). We should also begin
assembling the performance data and delivery records needed to substantiate the service-level shortfall in
mediation.

I am available to discuss at your convenience.

Dana
