MEMORANDUM
RE: Commercial Arrangements with Northwind Logistics Corporation - Status Update

TO: Management Committee
FROM: Corporate Development
DATE: April 7, 2025
SUBJECT: Status of Service Agreements and Related Filings

This memorandum summarizes the current status of our commercial arrangements with Northwind Logistics
Corporation ("Northwind") and related corporate and compliance matters, and requests a decision on next
steps.

1. Service Agreement and Performance

Under the Master Services Agreement dated June 1, 2024, Northwind provides dedicated fleet and brokerage
services to HarborPoint Holdings, Inc. On-time performance over the last quarter has averaged 82%, below the
95% service-level commitment. As discussed in the January 18 demand letter (a copy of which is attached), the
outstanding invoice balance is $218,440.00. Our outside counsel has advised that the cure period under
Section 9.4 of the agreement closes on February 6, 2025.

2. Proposed Amendments

Northwind has proposed an amendment that would (i) reduce the committed lane volume by 20%, (ii) extend the
term by eighteen months, and (iii) revise the pricing schedule to a blended per-mile rate. The attached draft
amendment (Exhibit A) has been reviewed by counsel, which has flagged two issues for attention: the
change-of-control provision and the proposed limitation of liability.

3. Corporate and Compliance Filings

Separately, the board approved at its January 10 meeting a resolution authorizing capital expenditures of up
to $1,500,000 in connection with the fleet expansion. The related annual report for the Delaware Secretary of
State was filed on March 31, 2025, and the amended and restated bylaws adopted on March 1, 2024 remain in
effect. The company also filed its Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 on February 27,
2025, reflecting net income of $16.1 million.

4. Decision Requested

Please advise whether management intends to (a) execute the proposed amendment, (b) exercise the termination
rights described in the demand letter, or (c) negotiate a further extension. A response is requested by April
14, 2025 so that we may prepare the appropriate documentation.

/s/ Corporate Development
Attachment: Exhibit A - Draft Amendment; copy of January 18 demand letter
